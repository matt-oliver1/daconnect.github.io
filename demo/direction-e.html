HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from sign-up to your first monthly report

All development data from every council in South Australia and the Gold Coast is received by DAconnect.

1
Choose what Council (or Councils) you want to work in
Choose the council areas you want — and get early access to every development opportunity before the rest of the market.
2
Enter your business details and create your digital business card
Build your professional profile with your logo, services, and contact information. You can update your details anytime.
3
Your digital business card is sent to all publicly listed developments
Your profile reaches decision-makers in your chosen council area. Exclusive list of no more than 14 businesses per council.
4
You receive a clear and full monthly report
Track your reach with detailed analytics showing exactly where your digital business card has been delivered.
