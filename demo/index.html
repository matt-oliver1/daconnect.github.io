Three design directions for the DAconnect homepage

Each is a complete, working homepage built with our real copy, real product screenshots and live development-application counts. Same content and same structure in all three — only the visual direction changes. Open each one, scroll it top to bottom, and tell us which one a builder would trust with their money.

Civic Tech

Assured · modern · spacious

The natural evolution of the brand we have today — navy-anchored, generous, product-grade polish. The safest option, and the one that stays most recognisably DAconnect.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque + Albert Sans

Open Civic Tech

Worksite

Bold · direct · industrial

Speaks the tradie's own language: hi-vis clarity, signage typography, hard edges, no decoration. The loudest option and the biggest departure from what we look like now.

Type: Archivo Expanded

Open Worksite

Dispatch

Live · technical · confident

Treats the product as what it actually is — an alert engine. Dark, precise, with the live lead feed as the hero. The wildcard, and the tightest-executed of the three.

Type: IBM Plex Sans + Mono

Open Dispatch

  • Each page has a small pill in the bottom-right corner to jump straight between the three directions.
